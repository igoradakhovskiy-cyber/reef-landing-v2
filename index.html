Life Office

Who looks after the apartment while you're away

Life Office, the resort's management company, takes care of the apartment between your visits: guests, cleaning, repairs and your belongings.

  1. Your dates. Stay whenever you want and as long as you want. Your family and friends too.
  2. You fly home. Life Office closes the apartment, stores your things and photographs where everything stood.
  3. Guests stay. Free dates go into the rental programme: short stays in the high season, longer stays in the quieter months.
  4. You come back. Everything is back in its place. Your kite and boards have been waiting at the lagoon all year (The Locker).

Guests can rent everything from a desk and monitor to a SUP or a baby cot, which makes stays of several weeks easy.

Where the guests come from

  • The beach club opens first. Pulse Bay opens in 2027, two years before the residences, and builds its own guest base.
  • Five source regions, different seasons. Europe in December–February and July–August, the Gulf in June–September, South Africa in December–January, Kenya all year.
  • Two wind seasons. Kaskazi from December to March, Kusi from June to September.

Location · East coast of Zanzibar

Michamvi, Zanzibar's sunset side

On most of the east coast the sun rises over the ocean. On the Michamvi peninsula it also sets into it. This is the quiet, premium end of the coast, home to some of the island's best-known hotels.

  • Sunset over the water, one of the few places on the east coast where you see it.
  • 81% of the peninsula's room stock is 5-star. Neighbours include Baraza and JAZ Elite Aurora.
  • 15 minutes to Paje, a top-30 kite spot in the world.
  • 75 minutes to Zanzibar airport.
  • 200 m to the ocean. A car-free courtyard, with buggies and shuttles inside the resort.

Source: Essence of Africa research, 2026.

An island resort that runs on its own power, water and internet

Power cuts and patchy internet are the first questions people ask about Zanzibar. Here is how the resort handles both.

Work-ready internet

  • Two separate links, satellite and mobile, so one outage doesn't take you offline.
  • Wi-Fi across the residence and the resort.
  • Soundproofed executive suites for calls and a quiet coworking room open 24 hours.
  • UTC+3: one hour from Dubai, one to two from Central Europe.
  • Power. Three sources: the grid, backup diesel generators and solar panels.
  • Water. Its own well and desalination, water treatment and reserve tanks. Hot water heated by the sun and by the air-conditioning.
  • Climate. Central cooling, with a cold-storage battery for peak hours.
  • Security. Guards 24/7, CCTV, keycard and QR access, fire alarms.
  • Environment. Wastewater treatment, composting, a car-free courtyard.
  • One control room watches pumps, chillers, lifts, generators, tanks and leaks, with a log of every incident.

FAQ

Common questions

Didn't find yours? Ask a manager on a call or in the messenger you prefer.

Who is the developer?

An international developer of premium real estate working full cycle, from construction to management: 8 projects in 3 countries and 814 keys under management. The company details and portfolio are in the presentation, and a manager will go through them with you on a call.

When will it be ready, and what opens before that?

The residences launch in 2029. Pulse Bay beach club opens first: the club in February 2027, sport and wellness in April 2027, restaurants and the evening programme by the end of 2027.

How does the payment plan work?

A refundable $1,000 booking fixes the unit and the price. Then 10% by the end of February 2027, 10% by 1 September 2027, and 8 quarterly payments of 10% from October 2027 to July 2029.

Is the $1,000 booking refundable?

Yes. It fixes your unit and price, and counts towards the first payment.

Can I stay as much as I want?

Yes. There is no limit on how long you, your family or friends stay. The dates you don't use can go into the rental programme.

Who rents it out when I'm away, and how am I paid?

Life Office, the resort's management company, handles guests, cleaning, check-in and maintenance. The programme terms, the management fee and payouts are set out in the management agreement, which comes with the presentation.

What exactly do I own?

The ownership structure, title documents and contract terms are in the presentation, and our manager goes through them with you before any payment beyond the booking.

Can I sell my unit before handover?

Resale and assignment before handover depend on the purchase contract. The contract terms come with the presentation, and a manager will walk you through what applies to your unit.

Does buying give me residency?

Units priced from $100,000 qualify to apply for a Tanzanian residence permit for you, your spouse and children. It is an immigration status, not citizenship or tax residency.

What about power, water and the internet?

The resort has its own well and desalination, three power sources (grid, backup generators and solar), central cooling, two separate internet links (satellite and mobile) with Wi-Fi across the resort, and a control room watching it all 24/7.

Is the beach club free for owners?

Owners get the Priority card at Pulse Bay: special prices on sport, wellness, the bar and restaurant, and priority booking for the best spots and events. You and your guests pay only for what you use.

How far is the beach, the airport and Paje?

200 m to the ocean, 15 minutes to Paje and 75 minutes to Zanzibar airport.
